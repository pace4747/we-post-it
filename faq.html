Frequently Asked Questions

Do we take your passwords?

No. We never take a password. You stay owner of Google and Facebook. After Google says yes, you add us as Manager, not Owner.

What's in $9.99 a month?

We get your Google (setup help, not sold), Facebook page, and one-page website started. We maintain them and post on Facebook for you. You text the photos to us. We post them for you. Same name, town, phone on Google, Facebook, Apple Maps, Bing, and your page.

Do they need a computer?

No. They text the photos to us. That is it. We do the rest. Need a change on the site. Text us.

Do we post if they send no photo?

A quiet week means nothing goes out. No photo, no post. They send photos when they have them. We space them out if they send a pile.

What if they already have Google?

If they already have a Google listing, we help them clean it up and add us as Manager so we can post photos. You keep your password. We never take it.

How often do we post?

At most 1 post per day and 2 posts per week on Facebook. We space out photos they send. If they send a big pile, we keep them and post over time.

Can they stop?

Yes. Stop whenever. Pay once and the website stays up for a year, even if you cancel.

Do we sell Google?

No. Google is setup help only. We sit with you on your phone. We type. You finish. You keep the password. You stay owner.

Do we do Instagram?

Instagram only if you ask. Then the same post caps as Facebook. At most 1 post per day and 2 posts per week.

Where are the rules?

The full rules are in the User Agreement on the Legal page. That is the agreement. Read it before you start.

If you got one customer from this, would it pay for itself for a year plus more? If you posted on Facebook weekly for a year, do you think you would get one customer? Yes.
