Questions about We Post It

Money

What is We Post It?

We Post It is a service for shop owners. We start your Google, Facebook page, and one-page website. We maintain them and post photos on Facebook for you. You text the photos. We post them. $9.99 a month. Stop whenever.

How much does it cost?

$9.99 a month. Stop whenever. Pay once and the website stays up for a year, even if you cancel.

Can they stop whenever?

Yes. $9.99 a month. Stop whenever. No contract. No commitment.

Is there a setup fee?

No. $9.99 a month. No setup fee. No hidden fees.

What if they stop paying?

We may pause work until payment is fixed. The website stays up for one year from the first payment, even if they cancel. After that year we may take it down. Google and Facebook stay theirs.

How it works

Do we take their passwords?

No. We never take a password. They stay owner of Google and Facebook. They add us as Manager, not Owner.

Is the website one page?

Yes. One page. Name, town, phone, hours, a photo. Not a store. Not extra pages. Not a blog.

Do they get a login with us?

No. There is no portal. No login. No dashboard. They text. We do the work.

Who texts the photos?

They do. They text the photos to us. We post them. Old ones are fine. A big pile is good. We space them out.

Can they send a lot of photos at once?

Yes. They can send a pile. Old photos are fine. We keep them and space them out over time. We post at most 1 photo a day, 2 photos a week.

What's the first photo for?

The first photo is the profile photo. It goes on Google, Facebook, the website, and everywhere else we put their name. Send a good one. A logo, the sign, the building, or the owner.

What if they need a change on the site?

Text us. We change the one-page site. At most 3 updates per day. An update is hours, phone, towns, words, a photo, or a look.

Do we make fake posts or promise a rank?

No. We use their photos and their words. We do not invent jobs, stones, prices, or captions. No fake posts. No rank promises.

What about Instagram?

Instagram only if they ask. Then the same post caps as Facebook. 1 post a day, 2 posts a week.

How do they get help?

The site chat is how-to-use-the-site only. Not a ticket. Not a lawyer. Legal questions point to /legal.

Do we work in Spanish?

Yes. The site has an ES button. Texts can be in Spanish. The website, Google, and Facebook can be in Spanish or English.

After they start

What happens to the site if they stop?

Pay once and the website we host stays up for one year from the first payment, even if they cancel. After that year we may take it down. Google and Facebook stay theirs.

Do they stay owner of Google and Facebook?

Yes. They stay owner. We never take a password. They add us as Manager, not Owner, so we can post photos.

How long until the shop is on Google?

We sit with them to start Google. That takes 15-30 minutes on their phone. Google may take days or weeks to verify. We stay with them until the proof is sent.

What if they already have Facebook?

If they already have a Facebook page for the shop, we can use that. They add us as Manager so we can post. If they do not have one, we start it.

What if they do not have a Gmail?

We make one on their phone during the Google sit-down. They pick the password. They keep it.

Do we host the site?

Yes. We host the one-page site. Pay once and it stays up for a year, even if they cancel.
