What's in $9.99 a month?

A one-page website, we start their Facebook page, and we post the photos they text. Google is setup help only. They come in just to finish.

Do they need a computer?

No. They text photos. We do the rest. They come in on their phone just to finish Google and Facebook.

Do we take your passwords?

No. Never. They stay owner.

Do we post if they send no photo?

No. One photo, one post. We do not invent jobs or captions.

What if they already have Google?

No. Setup help only. They stay owner. After Google says yes, they add us as Manager, not Owner.

Only if they ask.
