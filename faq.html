No. They text photos. We do the rest.

No. Never. They stay owner.

No. One photo, one post. We do not invent jobs or captions.

Yes. Old photos are fine. We organize them and space them out on the page.

At most 1 update per calendar week (Monday through Sunday, Central Time). An update pass can include photos you already sent plus one round of other changes: hours, phone, words on the page, a photo on the page, or taking a line off. You text us. We do the work.

No. Never. We build and host the site. They do not need to give us any passwords.
