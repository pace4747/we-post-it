This Cookie Notice explains how Sunset Gaming LLC doing business as Your Site uses cookies and similar storage on www.yoursite.site, shop pages on yoursite.site, and the login page. We do not run an advertising network. We do not use Meta, Google Ads, or other ad pixels.

What we use

  • Language (local storage). Remembers English or Spanish after you tap ES / EN. Not used for ads.
  • Start form ZIP (local storage). Remembers the last ZIP you typed so you do not retype it.
  • Login session (cookie). A signed cookie so you stay signed in on the login page and can change your shop page. We set it when you sign in. It is required for the login to work.
  • Visitor id (local storage). A first-party id on shop pages so two looks on the same phone can be one person. Used only to run the shop. Not an ad cookie. Not sent to Meta, Google Ads, or other ad networks.

We keep a shop-usage log of human looks, taps, login times, device type, and the connection's approximate city so we can run the shop. We may log an approximate city, ZIP, state, and country from the connection, the page you came from, timezone, and device type. We do not store your full IP address. We do not use GPS.

Stripe and other processors

When you pay $9.99 a month, Stripe runs the checkout. Stripe may set its own cookies on Stripe's pages. Those are Stripe's, under Stripe's privacy policy. If you Sign in with Google, Google may set cookies on Google's sign-in page. Those are Google's.

How to clear

Clear cookies and site data in your browser (often Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data). Clearing storage resets language to English and signs you out. The next visit starts fresh. You can also block cookies; login will not stay signed in.
