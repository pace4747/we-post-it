3. Information We Collect

This is also our notice at collection. We collect only what we need to run the Service.

3.1 Login

If you make a Your Site login, we collect your email and a hashed password. Your email is the login. We may send a confirm-email link so we know that inbox is yours. If you sign in with Google, Google sends us your name and a verified email. We do not receive your Google password. We use a signed session cookie so you stay signed in on your shop page. We do not run a public customer dashboard beyond the shop page and the login page.

3.2 Information You Provide to Us

  • Shop Information: Shop name, ZIP, and phone number. This is the minimum information required to start the Service.
  • Photos and Words: Photos you upload, and any captions or other text you add in Start or the editor.
  • Service Preferences: Hours of operation, additional towns or service areas, website look or palette preferences, and other information you enter about your shop.
  • Messages: If we already have a phone from sales outreach, the content of those texts. DIY shops reach us through Contact or login.
  • Feedback: If you use the Feedback page, the words you send and any phone or shop name you include.

3.3 Information We Do Not Copy From Other Platforms

The DIY Start wizard does not copy a Google Business Profile. If a shop came from outbound sales, we may have used public listing facts to start a draft. You remain the owner of any Google or Facebook account you already have. We never take a Google or Facebook password.

3.4 Payment Information

We use Stripe to handle credit card and debit card payments. Stripe collects and processes your payment card information. We do not store or have access to your full card number. We receive from Stripe a confirmation that you paid, the amount, the date, a receipt identifier, and the last four digits of the card for records and support. Stripe's use of payment information is governed by Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com.

3.5 Cookies, Local Storage, and Connection Notes

We use a signed session cookie so you stay signed in. We use browser local storage to remember English or Spanish and the last ZIP you typed on the Start form. Shop pages also keep a first-party visitor id in local storage (not an ad cookie) so two looks on the same phone can be one person. We keep a shop-usage log of human looks, taps (Call, Directions, Keep, Sign in), login times, device type, and the connection's approximate city so we can run the shop. We may log nearby city, ZIP, state, and country (approximate, from the connection), the page you came from, timezone, and device type. We do not store your full IP address. We do not use GPS. We do not use third-party cookies or ad pixels from Meta, Google Ads, or other ad networks. We do not sell visitor lists.

3.6 Do Not Track

We do not use advertising trackers. A "Do Not Track" signal in your browser does not change extra switches on this site, because there is no ad-tracking to turn off. Language and login cookies still work so the site can function.

3.7 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your Google password or Facebook password. If you make a Your Site login with a password, we store only a one-way hash, not the password itself. We do not collect your browsing activity on other websites. We do not collect precise device location. We do not collect biometric information. We do not run background checks or collect government-issued identification numbers.
